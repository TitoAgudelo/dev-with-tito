# Software Engineering Services — Tito Agudelo

## Overview

Tito Agudelo provides software engineering consulting and product development services for startups, growing businesses, and established engineering teams.

With more than 13 years of experience across mobile applications, web platforms, SaaS products, marketplaces, enterprise systems, and AI-enabled applications, Tito helps organizations design, build, improve, and maintain software products.

His approach combines hands-on development, practical architecture decisions, product thinking, and technical collaboration. He can contribute as an individual engineer, integrate into an existing team, or help guide technical decisions depending on the project's needs.

His primary areas of focus are React Native, React, TypeScript, full-stack development, application architecture, and AI product engineering.
