## Engineering Philosophy

### Architecture

Architecture is the quality of the decisions a team can keep making after the diagram is gone. Tito values stable contracts, explicit ownership, clear boundaries, and designs that allow individual parts of a system to evolve without unnecessarily affecting the rest.

He prefers pragmatic architecture that balances maintainability, delivery speed, reliability, and the actual needs of the product.

### Delivery and Quality

Tito treats accessibility, performance, security, and testing as part of the delivery process rather than cleanup tasks.

His approach includes:
- Short feedback loops through small, reviewable changes and frequent validation.
- Clear handling of loading, stale, unavailable, retryable, and successful states.
- Automated tests and type safety where they provide meaningful confidence.
- Observable failures and actionable error handling.
- Complexity that is justified by customer needs, operational reliability, or safer future changes.

He values shipping useful software while keeping it understandable, testable, and maintainable.

### Product Thinking and Collaboration

Tito considers engineering successful when it solves a real user or business problem. He works to understand requirements, communicate trade-offs, clarify scope, and identify risks early.

Through freelance consulting, he has collaborated directly with technical and nontechnical stakeholders, managed expectations, and helped clients make informed decisions about implementation, timelines, and budgets.

He believes strong engineers take ownership of outcomes, communicate clearly, and know when to simplify, investigate further, or ask for support.

## AI Engineering Philosophy

Tito approaches AI as both a product-design and reliability challenge. Probabilistic model output needs appropriate boundaries, relevant context, structured interfaces, observable retrieval, deterministic validation, safe fallbacks, and clear human ownership.

His AI-related work includes exploring and implementing LLM-powered product capabilities, designing prompts, integrating AI APIs, and developing retrieval-based experiences.

### Ask Tito AI

Ask Tito AI is a career-specific retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) assistant designed to answer questions about Tito's professional background, skills, projects, and engineering experience.

Its current retrieval architecture uses Google Gemini embeddings and Supabase with PostgreSQL and pgvector. Retrieved career information provides context for answer generation through an LLM provider.

The system is designed to ground answers in documented professional information rather than inventing projects, responsibilities, technologies, or outcomes.

### Public RAG Lab

The public RAG Lab is an educational project focused on understanding and demonstrating retrieval workflows, knowledge representation, and grounded AI responses.

It should be treated as a separate project from Ask Tito AI. Details about its implementation should be described independently rather than assumed to match Ask Tito AI's current technology stack.

Neither project should be presented as proof of undisclosed AI systems built for an employer or client.
