A major strength of Tito's Toptal experience is his ability to work directly with clients and stakeholders, including people with different levels of technical knowledge.

- Translating business needs into technical implementation plans.
- Explaining technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders in accessible language.

## Interview Talking Points

### Why have you worked with so many different companies?

I have been working through Toptal since 2016, so a significant part of my career has been freelance consulting. Toptal has connected me with different clients and projects over the years. I usually prefer long-term engagements, because I like understanding the product deeply and becoming a reliable part of the team. However, when a project ends or a client no longer needs the same support, I look for another suitable opportunity. That is why my work history includes several clients and sometimes overlapping dates.

### How do you handle clients who are not technical?

I focus first on understanding the business problem and what the client wants to achieve. Then I explain the technical options in terms of their impact, complexity, risks, and delivery expectations. I try to be transparent about what is realistic, identify dependencies early, and communicate clearly if the scope or assumptions change.

### How do you handle unrealistic client expectations?

I explain the technical reasons behind my concerns and discuss alternatives that could fit the available time and budget. I prefer to align expectations early instead of promising something that cannot be delivered responsibly. If we cannot reach a workable agreement, I communicate that clearly and use the appropriate escalation process when necessary.

### What makes your Toptal experience valuable?

It has given me the opportunity to work with different products, technologies, teams, and stakeholders. I have learned how to join unfamiliar codebases, identify where I can contribute, communicate directly with clients, and deliver value while adapting to different engineering environments.

### What has mentoring taught you?

Mentoring has reinforced the importance of explaining technical concepts clearly, understanding how other developers approach problems, and helping people improve through practical guidance. It has also given me an opportunity to contribute to the broader Toptal community.
