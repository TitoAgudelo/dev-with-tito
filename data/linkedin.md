# Tito Agudelo — Professional Profile

## Positioning

Tito is a systems-minded Staff Software Engineer and AI Architect working across web, mobile, platform, and pragmatic AI delivery. He helps teams turn complex product requirements into reliable software with clear interfaces, maintainable architecture, accessible experiences, and delivery systems that hold up beyond launch.

## Product environments

Public portfolio experience includes event ticketing, high-demand sports commerce, two-sided marketplaces, food technology, and enterprise process software. Work has crossed frontend, backend, mobile, cloud, and platform boundaries.

## Collaboration

Tito works well in small, accountable groups where product, design, and engineering share context early. He writes decisions down, surfaces tradeoffs before they become surprises, and keeps progress visible across distributed teams.

## Leadership

His leadership approach favors clarity over ceremony: define the outcome, make ownership explicit, reduce avoidable coupling, and give engineers enough context to make strong local decisions.
