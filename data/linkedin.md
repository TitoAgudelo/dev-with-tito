Tito's leadership approach favors clarity over ceremony:

- Define the desired outcome before selecting an implementation.
- Make ownership and system boundaries explicit.
- Reduce avoidable coupling and unnecessary complexity.
- Give engineers the context they need to make sound decisions.
- Use code reviews, testing, and feedback to improve quality continuously.
- Balance delivery speed with reliability and long-term maintainability.

His leadership combines hands-on contribution, architectural thinking, collaboration, and accountability for engineering outcomes.
